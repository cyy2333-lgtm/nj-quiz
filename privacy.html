隐私政策 Privacy Policy

最后更新 Last updated: 2026-11-07

本站是什么 About this site

njpermitquiz.com 是一个免费的新泽西驾照笔试练习网站。本站为个人练习工具，与新泽西州机动车委员会（MVC）无关。

njpermitquiz.com is a free practice site for the New Jersey driver knowledge test. It is not affiliated with the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC).

我们收集哪些信息 What we collect

不登录时：不收集任何个人信息。答题记录、错题本、收藏、笔记和设置只保存在你自己浏览器的本地存储（localStorage）中，清除浏览器数据即可删除。

Without an account: we collect no personal information. Your progress, wrong-answer list, favorites, notes and settings stay in your own browser (localStorage); clearing browser data removes them.

注册或登录后（可选）：账号由 Google Firebase Authentication 管理，我们会保存你的邮箱、昵称（或 Google 账号的显示名称）。密码由 Firebase 加密保存，我们看不到。你的错题本、收藏、笔记和练习记录会同步保存在 Google Cloud Firestore 中，只有你本人能读取。

With an optional account: accounts are handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your email and nickname (or your Google display name); passwords are stored by Firebase and are never visible to us. Your wrong-answer list, favorites, notes and progress are synced to Google Cloud Firestore and are readable only by you.

评论与留言：你发表的评论、留言和昵称会公开显示给所有访问者。你可以随时删除自己的内容；站长可以删除违规内容。请不要在评论里留下个人信息。

Comments and posts: comments and board posts are public, together with your nickname. You can delete your own at any time, and the site owner may remove inappropriate ones. Please do not include personal information.

"我考到过这题"：点这个按钮时，我们会记录你的账号 ID 和题号，用来统计考到过这题的人数。其他访问者只能看到总人数，看不到是谁点的。再点一次即可撤销。

"I got this on my test": tapping this button stores your account ID and the question number so we can count how many people saw that question. Other visitors see only the total, never who tapped it. Tap again to undo.

删除账号：如需删除账号及其数据，请在 GitHub Issues 联系我们（请勿在公开留言中写出你的邮箱，我们会通过其他方式核实）。

Account deletion: to delete your account and its data, contact us via GitHub Issues (do not post your email publicly; we will verify another way).

You may opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. 第三方服务 Third-party services

  • 账号、评论和"我考到过这题"由 Google Firebase（Authentication、Cloud Firestore）提供。
    Accounts, comments and "I got this on my test" are provided by Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore).
  • 点评论旁的翻译图标时，这条评论的文字会发送给 Google 翻译，以显示译文。不点就不会发送。
    When you tap the translate icon on a comment, that comment's text is sent to Google Translate to show a translation. Nothing is sent unless you tap it.

    Ads are provided by Google AdSense (see above).

    Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, so your browser connects to their servers.
