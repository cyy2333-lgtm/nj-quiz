隐私政策 Privacy Policy

本站是什么 About this site

njpermitquiz.com 是一个免费的新泽西驾照笔试练习网站。本站为个人练习工具，与新泽西州机动车委员会（MVC）无关。

njpermitquiz.com is a free practice site for the New Jersey driver knowledge test. It is not affiliated with the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC).

我们收集哪些信息 What we collect

本站没有账号系统，不要求你填写任何个人信息。你的答题记录、错题本、收藏和设置只保存在你自己浏览器的本地存储（localStorage）中，不会上传到我们的服务器。清除浏览器数据即可删除这些记录。

There are no accounts and we do not ask for personal information. Your progress, wrong-answer list, favorites and settings are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

第三方服务 Third-party services

  • 网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，域名解析由 Cloudflare 提供。它们可能为安全和运维记录访问日志（如 IP 地址）。
    The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Cloudflare DNS, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.
  • 字体由 Google Fonts 提供，加载字体时你的浏览器会连接 Google 服务器。
    Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers.
